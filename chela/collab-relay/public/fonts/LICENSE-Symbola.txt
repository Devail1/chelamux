`Symbola-Subset.ttf` in this directory is a subset of the upstream Symbola
font (Unicode ranges U+2300-23FF "Miscellaneous Technical" and U+2600-27BF
"Miscellaneous Symbols" + "Dingbats" only), produced with fontTools'
`pyftsubset` to keep the binary small. It is used exclusively as a
symbol-glyph fallback tier by `chela/telegram/screenshot.py`'s `/screenshot`
PNG renderer — it is not served to the browser and is not part of the web
terminal's font picker.
