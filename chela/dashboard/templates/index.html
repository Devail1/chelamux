A board for work in flight
The Kanban board tracks dispatched work across all your WORKFLOW.md files: open tasks, agents running now, PRs awaiting review, and done, all in one place. It fills in once the dispatcher has workflows to watch.
Enable it: set CHELA_DISPATCH_WORKFLOWS to a colon-separated list of WORKFLOW.md paths, then restart the daemon.
