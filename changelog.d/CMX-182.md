### Added

- **Turn timestamps as a band in the opt-in Mod.** With `CHELA_TERMINAL_TIMESTAMPS` on and
  `mods/chela-hooks/` loaded (Claude Code 2.1.301 or later), the time of the last reply is
  drawn above the prompt as `[HH:MM]`, in local time, instead of being written into the
  reply. The Mod tells the daemon through a field on each `MessageDisplay` payload, and the
  daemon then leaves the reply alone, so the time never shows twice. Without the Mod,
  `plugin/` stamps replies as before. (CMX-182)
